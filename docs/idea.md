# Lemonaid

A free, open-source discussion and issues board that any website owner can add to their site with one line.

## Who it is for

- **Website and product owners** who want one public place where their users can ask questions, share ideas and report problems. Each owner runs their own copy of Lemonaid.
- **Their visitors**: anyone on the internet can read everything, and anyone with an email address can join in.

## What it must do first
- Run your own copy and add the board to any website with one line.
- Read every topic and reply, with no need to sign in.
- Sign in with just your email and a one-time code sent to it.
- Start a topic labelled bug, idea or question, or reply to one, pasting text, links, code and screenshots.
- Upvote topics so the most wanted ones rise to the top, and see each issue's status (open, planned, fixed), which the owner sets.
- Get an email when someone replies to your post.
- Rude or harmful posts are stopped by a built-in bad-word check, with an AI check the owner can switch on. The owner can also delete posts and flag people who post inappropriate things.

## Not now
- One shared service that any website can sign up to, like Disqus.
- A comment box under each page, instead of a board.
- Attaching other files, like logs or PDFs.
- Search across all topics.
- A "report this post" button for visitors.
- More looks: a bright lemony theme, and a dark theme that follows the visitor's setting.
- One copy serving several different websites.

## How it should feel
Clean and plain, so it fits into any website without clashing. The owner picks one colour to match their site.
Quick and friendly to use: posting should take seconds, and signing in should never feel like a chore.
